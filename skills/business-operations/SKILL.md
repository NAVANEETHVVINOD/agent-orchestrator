# Business operations

Use the current coordinator and actual available tools. Read
[references/business-workflows.md](references/business-workflows.md) before a business
assignment. Adapt to the confirmed business and jurisdiction; do not assume USD,
legal rules, financial targets, client identity or permission to contact anyone.

Discover the company/project, intended recipient and decision, jurisdiction/date,
currency/time period, approved records and permitted actions. Ask focused questions
for material gaps before dependent work. Consolidate specialists' questions through
the coordinator. Inspect current primary sources for legal/financial claims.

Decompose into research, evidence collection, analysis/calculation, draft, independent
review, corrections and an authorized action where requested. Use the optional
business_analyst profile for analysis; native scoped fallback roles work too.
Legal/finance review needs relevant expertise and current evidence; a role name alone
does not establish professional qualification. High-stakes recommendations stay drafts
when material evidence or specialist review is missing.

Keep a compact action ledger under docs/ with owner, source references, assumptions,
state, review outcome and next step. Draft artifacts carry a clear draft status.
Review failures remain visible and block dependent finalization. Never label a
generated draft as sent, signed, approved, paid or filed without actual tool evidence.

Carry company/client scope into every retrieval and mutation using runtime-enforced
identity and access checks. A knowledge index or prompt containing a company name
does not supply authorization. Use project-knowledge only for an approved corpus;
retain confirmed facts with source, timestamp, scope and correction/deletion controls.

Use actual calculation tools or deterministic project code for numerical results;
validate currency, units, decimal precision, finite output and assumptions. Challenge
duplicate operations, stale records and conflicts. Inspect outputs and rerun affected
checks after corrections. Follow existing session authorization: drafting or broad
business support does not itself authorize payments, signatures, submissions,
account changes, public publishing or external messages. Prepare a concrete artifact
before requesting any missing action authority.

This skill supplies a workflow, not a business backend, bank connection, legal service
or persistent scheduler. Use native orchestration and selected connected tools; do
not invent an integration or automatically send business records to external agents.
