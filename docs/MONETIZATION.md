# Business options and current plugin limits

Policy checked 2 October 2026. Publishing does not establish an entitlement to developer
payouts; no general install-based payment program was identified in the reviewed docs.

Current policy restricts plugin commerce to physical goods. Digital-service sales,
subscriptions, credits and freemium upsells are prohibited through the plugin. Existing
paid-account features may be accessed, but the plugin cannot initiate subscriptions,
promote upgrades or link to checkout. See [OpenAI policy](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/plugin-guidelines)
and [checkout scope](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/build/monetization).

A possible business model is the Apache-2.0 core plus independently marketed setup,
support or enterprise hosting. A future authenticated service requires separate design
and review. This package contains no billing, tracking or upsell mechanism. Recheck
policy before launching a paid integration; neither revenue nor approval is guaranteed.
